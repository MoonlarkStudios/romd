# Content-rating mark attribution

Content-rating marks are credited to their respective authorities:

- Australian Classification Board (ACB)
- Computer Entertainment Rating Organization (CERO)
- Classificação Indicativa
- Entertainment Software Rating Board (ESRB)
- Game Rating and Administration Committee (GRAC)
- Pan European Game Information (PEGI)
- Unterhaltungssoftware Selbstkontrolle (USK)

ROMD and Ottercade use these marks to display the classification recorded for a catalog
title. Their inclusion does not imply affiliation or endorsement. The marks
retain their owners' rights and are not covered by the project's AGPL license.

See [Credits and Third-Party Notices](../../../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md#content-rating-marks)
for the central inventory and official authority links, and
[Trademark Policy](../../../../TRADEMARKS.md).
