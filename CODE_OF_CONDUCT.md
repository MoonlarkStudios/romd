# Code of conduct

ROMD welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience and perspectives.
Everyone participating in project issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project spaces is expected to help keep them respectful and useful.

## Participate with care

- Treat people with respect. Critique ideas and behavior, and explain technical
  disagreements with evidence.
- Make room for questions, differing experience and accessibility needs.
- Respect boundaries, privacy and requests to stop unwelcome contact.
- Keep contributions relevant to the project and follow maintainer moderation.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation,
deliberate disclosure of private information, and repeated disruptive behavior
are unacceptable. These expectations also apply when someone represents ROMD
outside its project spaces.

## Report a concern privately

Email **[john@moonlarkstudios.com](mailto:john@moonlarkstudios.com)** for conduct
reports. Include links to the relevant public content and enough context to
understand the concern. Share sensitive evidence only as needed; do not post
allegations, personal information or private correspondence in public issues.

Reports are reviewed by the project maintainer. Information is shared only as
needed to assess and address the concern. A single-maintainer project cannot
promise an independent investigation or a particular response time. GitHub's
own reporting tools are also available for abuse on its platform.

Report software vulnerabilities or exposed credentials through
[the security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may clarify expectations, edit or remove content, close a discussion,
limit participation, or temporarily or permanently exclude a participant.
Responses should reflect the severity, context and repetition of the behavior.
Participants should respect privacy while a concern is reviewed and comply with
moderation decisions. Corrections or additional context can be sent through the
same private reporting address.
